Paper Sky

Parker and Emma fold a paper airplane — and it takes them everywhere. A 72-second risograph cartoon with its own music.

Download the video Open the live, frame-by-frame version How it was made

The adventure

  1. 0:00 At their desk, Emma and Parker take turns folding a doodled sheet into a dart.
  2. 0:07 Emma throws it; it loops the classroom and zooms straight at us.
  3. 0:13 A cloud parts — they're riding the giant plane over town, loop-the-loop and all.
  4. 0:25 Inside a big top floating on a cloud, the plane flattens into a trapeze.
  5. 0:38 Through the jungle with a macaw, a waving monkey and a waterfall — then poof, a paper hot-air balloon.
  6. 0:51 Sunset over town and school, back into a plane, through the window…
  7. 1:03 …and home onto the desk. Peace sign, thumbs up.
Every pixel and note is generated in code (Canvas 2D and Web Audio) in a single HTML file, using the riso-windowseat print engine and sound kit. The live version runs slower than real time on most machines; the video is the smooth way to watch.
